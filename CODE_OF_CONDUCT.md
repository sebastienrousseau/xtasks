# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about
the work, not the person doing it.

## Expected

- Give feedback on the change, with a reason attached.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without it being a
  judgement of you.
- Say when you are unsure.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or persistently pushing a rejected change
after a decision has been made and explained.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to <sebastian.rousseau@gmail.com>. Reports are handled
privately. Responses range from a request to change behaviour through to
a permanent ban, depending on severity.
